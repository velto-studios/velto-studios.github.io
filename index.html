Process

A paid two-week pilot. The work speaks first.

Every engagement starts the same way: a scoped slice of your real backlog, built in two weeks. If it speaks for itself, we continue. If not, you keep the code and walk away - no retainer, no lock-in.

01

Discover

One call. We map the problem, the constraints and what done looks like.

02

Strategize

We agree a narrow, buildable slice - something that matters but is not on your critical path.

03

Design

Flows, interfaces and architecture, decided before code. No surprises mid-build.

04

Engineer

The build. A written update lands in your inbox every working day.

05

Deploy

Working software in your hands, with docs and a walkthrough.

06

Optimize

Measure, tighten, improve. If the pilot speaks for itself, we scope the next build.
